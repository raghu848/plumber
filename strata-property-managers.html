Strata and property management

A plumbing partner for managed properties

Managing a building means dealing with plumbing faults quickly, keeping owners informed and making sure the right party pays for the right work. Hydrolink Plumbing supports strata managers, property managers and commercial property owners across Sydney with licensed plumbers (licence no. 265578C) who are used to working with managed buildings.

We have built working relationships with strata management teams across Sydney, and we currently carry out routine maintenance and emergency work for strata firms. To talk through your portfolio, call 0433 629 517 or send a quote request below.

Plumbing services for managed properties

  • Grease trap installation and maintenance
  • Backflow prevention device installation and maintenance
  • Hot water system installation, repair and replacement
  • Burst pipe repairs and water leak detection and repair
  • Blocked drain clearing and pipe relining
  • Roof and gutter repairs and replacement
  • Repair and replacement of leaking taps and toilets

Common areas and individual units

Plumbing in a managed building is split between common property and individual lots. Our plumbers understand what responsibilities sit with occupants and what sits with the strata, and we make sure no unnecessary work is carried out. That helps keep costs fair for whoever is responsible for them.

  • Common areas: shared pipework, drainage, hot water, roof and gutter drainage, and garage and plant areas.
  • Individual units: taps, toilets, leaks and drains reported by owners or tenants, with access arranged through you.

Emergency plumbing assistance

Burst pipes, overflowing drains and hot water failures in a managed building affect many people at once. We provide an emergency service for homes, businesses and strata properties. Call 0433 629 517, tell us what is happening and we will explain the next steps. More detail is on our emergency plumbing page.

Maintenance and repair

Routine maintenance helps catch problems before they become emergencies. Our work starts with an onsite consultation to assess what the property needs, and the consultation fee is credited toward works. We can set up routine maintenance with your team and respond to repair requests as they come in.

Communication and coordination

  • Clear updates. We keep managers informed about the progress of each job.
  • Access and approvals. We work to your process for access, tenant notice and work approval.
  • Plain reporting. You get a straightforward explanation of the cause, the fix and any follow-up.
  • Budget awareness. We aim to complete work to a high standard and within budget.

Strata and property manager FAQs

  • What do you do for strata managers and owners corporations?
    We carry out plumbing repairs and maintenance for strata buildings, covering common property such as shared pipework, drains, hot water systems and roof and gutter drainage. We also work on routine maintenance contracts and emergency call-outs for strata firms. See our strata plumbing page for the services we provide.
  • Can you fix a problem inside a single unit?
    Yes. We can attend individual lots as well as common areas. Before we start, we work out whether the fault sits in the lot or in common property, because that decides who is responsible for the cost. We try to make sure no unnecessary work is carried out so the expense is not passed to the wrong party.
  • Who do you report to and how do you keep us updated?
    We keep the property manager or strata manager informed as the job progresses, including what we found, what we recommend and what has been completed. Tell us who should be contacted for access and who approves the work, and we will work to that.
  • Do you offer emergency plumbing for managed buildings?
    Yes. We handle emergency plumbing for homes, businesses and strata properties. If water is escaping or a drain has backed up, call 0433 629 517 and describe the problem. Our emergency plumbing page explains what to do while you wait.
  • Do you provide a quote before work begins?
    Our services start with an onsite consultation to assess what is needed, and that consultation is credited toward the works. Use the quote form below or call us and we will arrange it.
  • Which areas do you cover?
    We work across Sydney, including the north-west, Parramatta, the Hills District, Ryde and the Inner West. Visit our service areas page to check your suburb.
