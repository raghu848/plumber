Toilet Repairs Sydney Faq's

  • How Much Does It Cost To Get A New Toilet Installed?
    This question will have a different answer every time. A few things that a professional Sydney plumber would need to consider before coming up with prices. First, is this a new installation or a replacement toilet? A new installation means that the plumbing needs to be perfect, and the hired contractor would have already included the installation cost in the bill. For toilet replacement, it's best to call our toilet expert team for advice before getting one. Other things to consider are the type and location of the property, the brand and type of the toilet, the condition of your existing drainage and plumbing, and the time the qualified Sydney plumbers spend on the project. It is best to contact our skilled plumbers at Hydrolink Plumbing Services for expert advice and better estimates.
  • How Often Should Toilets Be Replaced?
    The typical lifespan of a toilet is 25 years; thus, this can be used as a benchmark. In some circles, it is claimed that the porcelain toilet bowl itself has a considerably longer and even infinite lifespan. It is only the inside components that need to be repaired and replaced.
  • What kind of broken toilets can you fix?
    Toilet not flushing: If your toilet is constantly running or won't flush properly, you're going to need to call in your local expert plumber. There are several reasons that your toilet is not flushing – most commonly, the problem is within the fill valve or the flapper inside the toilet tank or a blockage in the sewer line.

    Leaking toilet: The cistern (the tank that holds the water that flushes your toilet) can leak, causing water wastage and a hazard for your bathroom. A leaking cistern is relatively common, is caused by damage or wear or tear over time, and can easily be repaired or replaced by Hydrolink Plumbing.

    Blocked toilet: Is your toilet clogged up, maybe even overflowing? A blocked toilet is one of the most common complaints we remediate with Sydney customers. A build-up of waste, toilet paper, or other items thrown in the toilet can cause toilet blockages. If you're facing a blocked toilet, give Hydrolink Plumbing a call today, and our plumbers will unclog your plumbing and remediate the problem.
  • Can you replace my old toilet?
    We sure can. In many cases, if you're in an older Sydney home, a damaged toilet means it's not possible to save your old equipment, and it's time for a new one. Nothing better than a new loo! We offer toilet installation, offering your home or commercial property a high-quality, long-lasting, efficient toilet that will save water consumption while providing superior performance when compared to your old, leaky, or faulty toilet.
