Gas heating

Gas heater services from Hydrolink Plumbing

A gas heater works hard through the cooler months, and it should do so quietly and safely. When it stops lighting, smells odd or just isn’t keeping the room warm, it is worth having it looked at properly rather than putting up with it. Hydrolink Plumbing looks after gas heaters for Sydney homes and businesses, from a routine check through to repairs and new installations.

Our plumbers are licensed (licence no. 265578C) and are qualified to work on gas appliances, so one visit can cover the heater, the gas connection and any related plumbing. If you are not sure what the problem is, call 0433 629 517 and describe what is happening. We will tell you what we would check first.

What we can help with

  • Gas heater servicing. A check of the heater and its gas connection so it runs safely and as the manufacturer intended.
  • Gas heater repairs. Finding the cause of a heater that will not light, keeps shutting off or is not performing as it should, then fixing it or explaining your options.
  • Gas heater installation and replacement. Fitting a new heater, or replacing an old one, including the gas connection. We look at the gas supply, positioning and ventilation first.
  • Gas connections and fittings. Pipework, isolation valves and connections for heaters and other appliances. See our gas fitting service.

Common gas heater concerns

  • The pilot light will not stay lit, or the heater is slow to start.
  • A yellow or orange flame, or soot and scorch marks near the heater.
  • The heater turns on and off, or does not warm the room as well as it used to.
  • Unusual noises or smells.
  • Condensation or damp on nearby windows and walls.
  • An older heater and uncertainty about whether it is worth repairing or time to replace it.

If you can smell gas, do not use switches or flames, leave the building and call your gas network’s emergency line or 000 first. Then call us once the gas has been made safe. Do not try to repair a gas heater yourself: gas work in New South Wales must be carried out by a licensed tradesperson.

Why professional servicing matters

  • Safety. A faulty heater can leak gas or produce carbon monoxide. A regular check helps catch problems early.
  • Reliable heating. Finding a worn part now is easier than going without heat in the middle of a cold week.
  • Efficiency. A heater that burns properly makes better use of the gas you pay for.
  • Longer working life. Small issues dealt with early are less likely to become larger repairs.
  • Clear advice. You get an honest explanation of whether to repair or replace, so you can decide with the facts in front of you.

Related gas and plumbing services

Gas heater FAQs

  • How often should a gas heater be serviced?
    Follow the manufacturer’s recommendation for your model. Many manufacturers suggest a check every one to two years, and an older heater, or one that gets heavy use, may benefit from more regular attention. Booking before the cold weather arrives means any problem is found while it is still easy to fix.
  • What are the signs my gas heater needs attention?
    Look out for a yellow or orange flame instead of a steady blue one, soot or scorch marks around the heater, a pilot light that keeps going out, a heater that is slow to light or cycles on and off, odd smells or noises, and condensation on nearby windows. Any of these is a good reason to stop using the heater and have it looked at.
  • What should I do if I can smell gas?
    Do not use switches, lighters or appliances. Open windows and doors if it is safe, leave the building and call your gas network’s emergency line (for most Sydney homes this is Jemena on 131 909) or call 000 if you are in danger. Once the gas is made safe, call us on 0433 629 517 and we can look at the cause. You can read more on our gas leak detection page.
  • Can you install a new gas heater?
    Yes. Installation is part of our gas fitting work. Before we start, we look at the gas supply, the position of the heater and its ventilation or flue needs so the heater is installed safely and to the manufacturer’s instructions. Tell us the make and model you have in mind when you call or send an enquiry.
  • Do you work on heaters in rentals, strata buildings and businesses?
    We work on gas appliances in homes and businesses. If the property is rented or part of a strata scheme, let us know who looks after maintenance so we can sort out access and approvals. See our strata plumbing and commercial plumbing pages for more.
  • How do I get a quote?
    Call 0433 629 517 or use the quote form on this page. The more you can tell us about the heater (type, make, age and what is going wrong), the easier it is to explain what is involved. We do not guess at costs without knowing the job.
