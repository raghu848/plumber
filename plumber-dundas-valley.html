Plumbing questions from Dundas Valley customers

  • How can I tell if there is an underground pipe leak at my Dundas Valley home?
    Signs include a rising water bill with no change in use, soggy ground, a constantly running meter, or reduced pressure. We can use leak detection to locate the leak before any digging.
  • Which type of hot water system suits an extended home?
    It depends on household size, space and your energy source. We can compare gas, electric, heat pump and solar options with you and recommend what suits your plans.
  • Should I call a plumber before my builder starts?
    Yes, it helps. Early advice on pipe positions, fixtures and gas or water loads often avoids changes mid-build.
