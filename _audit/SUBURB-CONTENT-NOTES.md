## What changed on every page
- **Replaced:** the "About" intro block, the stack of about 20 keyword-repeated service blurbs, and the old FAQ accordion (which contained claims such as "100% satisfaction guarantee" and "competitive rates", plus "Norwest siders"-style stuffing).
- **New per suburb:** a local intro, two paragraphs on the property types and plumbing needs of that suburb (residential, strata or commercial as relevant), a linked list of 8 relevant services, three FAQs (shown in the existing accordion), a clear call/quote CTA, and links to `emergency.html`, `areas.html` and `contact.html`.
- **Head:** unique title and meta description; the meta keywords tag is removed; JSON-LD now has a `Plumber` entity (business name, phone, Parramatta address, 24-hour opening, the suburb as area served), a `WebPage` and an `FAQPage`. No ratings or reviews are in the schema.
- **Services tabs ("Services We Provide"):** suburb names removed from these shared blurbs (1,920 mentions across the 40 pages), so they read as general service text rather than repeated keywords.
- **Kept as is:** header, banner, quote form, footer, map, styling and scripts.
- **Result:** about 220 to 270 words of new unique text per page. The average 5-word-phrase overlap between any two pages is about 12% (highest 18%). The page body dropped from about 6,600 to about 1,450 words.
